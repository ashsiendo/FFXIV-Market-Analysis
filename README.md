# FFXIV Market Dynamics

*Gil in Motion Across NA Worlds*

**How differently do FFXIV markets actually move from world to world?**

I started this project after exploring different North American worlds in Final Fantasy XIV while thinking about making a transfer to a new home world and learning how to deal with the Market Board economy when I first joined the game in 2026.

FFXIV has a unique player-driven economy, with each world having its own MB, plus cross-world buying, gathering, crafting, and retainer selling. As I learned how to make gil, I became curious about how the same items might behave differently on each world and whether prices alone explain everything.

This project uses Universalis data to poke around FFXIV's NA markets and examine variations in prices, sales activity, supply, seller competition, and behavior within item categories. It seeks to understand how these markets change over time, how they are different from one another, and what those differences would imply for people who are buying, collecting, crafting, and selling items, instead of aiming to determine a single universally "best" or cheapest market.

## Current Study (Study 1)
*Late-Dawntrail Selected-World Market Snapshot and EDA*

We will compare four selected NA worlds, with their Data Centers in parentheses: 
**Goblin** (Crystal) as the personal reference world; **Famfrit** (Primal); **Jenova** and **Adamantoise** (both Aether).

The current study concentrates on October-centered, late-2026 MB data, recording the exact dates when the data was collected. It examines current listings on the MB alongside recent sales history over a consistent lookback period.

It also provides early insights into certain world markets in the later stages of the Dawntrail expansion, and later research could involve looking at more worlds, monitoring changes over time, studying behavior during the forthcoming Evercold expansion, and exploring merchant-related questions.
